This login can only watch. Sign out and connect with one that can send commands.

Each button runs just that step, with the same checks as the automatic runs, and is refused while that tank is busy. Rinse stops at the dirty tank's sensor and leaves its water in the tank until you drain it. Fill doesn't add cleaner. Stop turns everything off and holds the automatic runs until Resume.
